    Function followedOrderIssues

    • followedOrderIssues(
          agreement: Agreement,
          commitment: Commitment,
          design: HydratedProcessDesign | null,
          followed: readonly FollowedOrderRead[],
      ): CommitmentAgreementIssue[]

      The counter-signer's check of the orders an agreement follows against the process design it names, over the counter-signer's own reads (followed). The pure sign gate holds the topology leaf's form and its number of edges (validateCommitmentAgreement); this check needs the chain, so it sits with the party that reads it, before that party signs: the seller at accept, once the orders before its own are committed.

      An order instantiates a design entry of the registered agreement its provenance names that follows as many entries as its leaf names orders. For that entry, each order the leaf names, in the entry's order: is recorded on the chain (a read in followed), in the process the commitment joins (processId), and its agreement, read and checked, names the same assembly and the registered agreement of the entry the design entry follows at that place. The first entry every edge matches passes; when none does, the first entry's issues are returned. An agreement with no provenance or no topology leaf, a design not in hand, and an agreement no entry matches by count are the sign gate's to refuse, and return no issue here.

      • agreement: Agreement
      • commitment: Commitment
      • design: HydratedProcessDesign | null
