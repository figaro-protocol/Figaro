# The site briefs — approved by the maintainer

THE FOREST (lead every telling with it; the layman never meets it in these words):
Figaro is a whole new economic system that displaces, but does not completely substitute, the
institutions humanity has depended on for thousands of years, with math and for the price of gas.
The process is the institution; it lasts the time of one transaction. Institutions are ex-post;
Figaro is ex-ante. Every asset is a wallet address. Two bonds is the illustration, never the limit.
What changes: from occult institutions that support a hierarchy to flat, transparent, verifiable
processes. The data is for regulatory, legal, fiscal, and market-making purposes first; analysis last.

THREE READERS, THREE NAV SECTIONS (verbs): Participate (laymen = members = users), Build
(builders), Research (academics). The docs site (fumadocs) is for builders who BUILD; not here.

RULES FOR EVERY PAGE: one idea; the cap is a ceiling; one figure or table at most; one link
onward; positive form (state what is, never what is not); no live counts anywhere; no tools on
a marketing page; no network names (write "an EVM-compatible chain"; never Ethereum as THE chain);
no status/launch language; no eyebrows; lexicon nouns only where the brief allows them and in
plain words on first use (docs/LEXICON.md is the one list); every claim traces to THEORY.md, the
kernel, or a named paper — a claim that does not trace does not ship; no loss-ratio claims; no
dominance-grammar restatement ("cooperation is dominant") — after performance resolving beats
never resolving for the buyer, and given that performing is the seller's best response.

## HOME (100 words)
Tagline "My word is my bond". Two-sentence lead in plain words: trade directly with anyone,
anywhere; your word is your bond, and the math holds it. Three doors, one per reader:
"Participate" → /discover (the app); "Build" → /institutions; "Research" → /working-groups.
Nouns: bond. No figure. Nothing else.

## PARTICIPATE — the layman's pages (register: plain words a member uses; NO mechanism, NO
institutions, NO network, NO florin, NO what-changes; nouns: bond, member)
1. /your-word-is-your-bond (150): You trade directly with a stranger. Both put up a bond bigger
   than the trade. Nobody holds it, nobody is in the middle. You get yours back when you close and
   the seller is paid. It costs gas, cents to dollars. Figure: the lockbox — two bonds in, close,
   two bonds out. Sources: the kernel page's lockbox telling; FAQ digest (who holds, what you put
   up). Onward: /when-something-goes-wrong.
2. /when-something-goes-wrong (180): Nobody is paid until you say the trade is done, so a problem
   is fixed first, then you close. The other sellers want it fixed too — they wait on the same
   close. If you truly disagree, the forum you chose or an ordinary court reads the data; nothing
   on chain decides for you. Never close and your own bond stays locked too. Figure: the five
   layers as five plain lines. Sources: FAQ non-delivery, genuine disagreement, what do I lose.
   Onward: /your-agent.
3. /your-agent (130): Software can trade for you with your wallet and your rules: buy, sell,
   confirm a delivery, within limits you set. It uses Figaro the way you do. Keep your key safe;
   the leash is yours. No figure. Sources: /agents opening; actor-neutral paper's policy section in
   plain words. Onward: /your-data.
4. /your-data (180): Your books close themselves. Every trade leaves you a receipt, an invoice,
   your evidence and your tax trail, kept by you and provable to anyone. Public is the map — who
   traded with whom, for how much; your detail stays yours. You can sell your data on your terms
   and the buyer can check it is real. Figure: two columns, public / yours. Sources: /data
   public-private table; FAQ tax-and-law; self-authenticating-data-sales in plain words.
   Onward: /your-communitys-token.
5. /your-communitys-token (130): A trade uses one token, chosen by the people in it, and any of
   these will do: a stablecoin; the chain's own coin; your community's own token, wherever you
   are, with anyone who takes it; the token a market was built with, when its designer chose one.
   Figure: one table, the kinds of token and who chooses each. Sources: VISION "Where the value
   goes"; florin paper §7; the utility-token clause, in plain words. Onward: /become-a-member.
6. /become-a-member (130): Buying needs only a wallet. To sell, publish a profile — what you
   offer and where — post a stake you get back when you leave, and you appear on Discover. People
   and software register the same way. The page LINKS TO THE REGISTRATION WIZARD (`/members/identity`,
   its first step) as its one action. No figure. Sources: /members opening; the wizard steps.
   Onward: /discover.
7. /faq (no cap): USING Figaro only. Who holds the tokens; refunds; change of mind; lost keys;
   what is public about me; can an agent act for me; can the site lie about what I sign (one line
   → /audit); can anyone freeze it; tax and law (one line). Each answer: the concern, what the
   protocol does, the residual, one link to its owner page. Source: current /faq consumer half,
   cut of everything it re-derives. Onward: /discover.

## BUILD — the builder's pages (register: the displacement and the mechanism, stated as fact)
1. /institutions (200): Every institution did one job — make a stranger's promise worth acting
   on — and did it ex-post, by authority (guild court, state, platform). Figaro does it ex-ante:
   the bond is locked before the trade. Displaced, not substituted; firm, platform, court keep the
   regions where they still win. Nouns: process. Figure: table guild/state/platform/math — who
   makes the promise good, and when. Sources: coordination-substrates, code-is-constitution,
   coercion-variable. Onward: /the-process.
2. /the-process (250): Mechanism design — the rules are public, the equilibrium is a theorem,
   publishing the rules completes the mechanism. The process is the institution: N value-adders
   bond in, the buyer closes, all paid and refunded at once, it dissolves leaving its own books.
   Two bonds is the illustration; the limit is the network's — about 1,240 orders per process on a
   30M-gas block, more after Glamsterdam. One honest sentence: verified six ways by the project
   itself, not yet audited by an outside party, nothing to pause or upgrade, full account in the
   docs. Nouns: order, resolution. Figure: the outcome table. Sources: asymmetric-bonding, THEORY,
   disclosure-asymmetry, self-closing-ledger, the verified-kernel paper. Onward: /the-network.
3. /the-network (200): Many buyers and many chains at once — every seller is the buyer of its own
   process for its own inputs, so processes link into chains of processes — and trades formed
   with no venue in the middle. Every asset is a wallet address, trading for itself under the same
   terms in every jurisdiction. Nouns: assembly. Figure: the process-topology figure. Sources:
   asymmetric-bonding §5, markets-without-a-venue, transaction-scoped-institutions,
   wallet-legal-subject. Onward: /agents-that-design.
4. /agents-that-design (200): Software wallets on the same footing as people, here as designers:
   writing clauses, composing assemblies, operating members, on the operator SDK under a policy of
   the owner's choosing. Nouns: clause, designer. Figure: asset / wallet / operator. Sources:
   actor-neutral-coordination, docs/AI_AGENT_COORDINATION.md, ecosystem-agents/*.md.
   Onward: /the-data-layer.
5. /the-data-layer (250): The chain holds a fingerprint; the detail stays with its producer.
   Every process is a self-closing ledger: the regulatory data is a retrieval, the signed data
   is evidence, receipts route at resolution, and the public map with published prices is what
   makes markets form. The data market is an ordinary bonded sale. Nouns: attestation, registry.
   Figure: table regulatory/legal/fiscal/market-making — what the data gives. Sources:
   self-closing-ledger, coordination-substrates §3.8, on-chain-evidence, markets-without-a-venue,
   docs/DATA_LAYER.md, self-authenticating-data-sales. Onward: /the-token.
6. /tokens-you-build-with (200): A designer pins ANY ERC-20 as the unit an assembly trades in:
   a stablecoin, the chain's own asset, a community's token, or the designer's OWN token, whose
   worth is discovered by use of what they built — the share certificate of an entity never
   incorporated. One token per process; a swap before commit is the on-ramp. Nouns: denomination,
   utility token. Figure: table — token kind, who chooses it, what it says. Sources: VISION "Where
   the value goes", the Schelling-point paper §7, `clauses/figaro-utility-token.json`,
   `assemblies/equipment-hire.json`, protocol-composition §6 (the swap as on-ramp).
   Onward: /the-token.
7. /the-token (200): Figaro's own tokenomics. One billion florins, fixed. 600M for designers,
   paid by a published formula on adoption counted on chain. 300M for the DAO. 100M at genesis. A
   Schelling point: no yield, no vote, no privilege at resolution. Nouns: florin, treasury. Figure:
   the supply split. Sources: florin paper, docs/FLORIN_TOKEN.md, docs/DESIGNER_REWARDS.md,
   docs/DAO.md. Onward: the docs site (link placeholder `/docs`).
