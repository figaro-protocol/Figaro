# Figaro Protocol — Framing

*How to explain Figaro as a cryptoeconomic coordination system.*

---

## The argument

Bitcoin showed that a set of rules, baked into code, accepted by all participants,
could coordinate thousands of independent nodes into a system that provides security
and service without any authority giving orders. The three things that authority in
organizations exists to produce — cooperation, collaboration, communication — emerged
from the mechanism itself. That's what permissionless means: not "no rules," but
"no ruler required."

Leadership in organizations exists *because* cooperation, collaboration, and
communication don't happen automatically between strangers. The mechanism makes them
happen automatically. When the mechanism does what leadership does, you no longer
need permission from leadership to participate.

Ethereum extended this to programmable computation. Others have extended it to
lending, trading, governance.

We extended it to trade: to the N-party value-added process between strangers.
Figaro is a cryptoeconomic system in which the bonding mechanism produces cooperation
between parties who've never met, at any scale, in any jurisdiction. Not by reducing
the cost of a transaction. By reducing the need for the institutional structures that
coordination used to require.

The result is permissionless trade — the handshake between strangers, restored at
protocol scale. TCP/IP for trade.

---

## Why this framing works

**The 3 Cs derivation of permissionlessness.** Most explanations of permissionless
systems describe the absence of gatekeepers. This framing explains the *mechanism
that makes gatekeepers unnecessary*. Those are different arguments. The second one
is convincing in a way the first isn't.

**The lineage positions Figaro correctly.** Bitcoin → Ethereum → Figaro reads as
three cryptoeconomic systems in the same category — not a dapp built on top of
another thing. This is the right register for both crypto-native and institutional
audiences.

**"Inherit EVM security, scale via coordination mechanisms"** is a precise statement
of the architectural choice that distinguishes Figaro from scaling projects without
dismissing them.

**"Restore the handshake between strangers"** is the right emotional frame. Not
"remove the middleman" (defines by subtraction). Not "trustless" (a technical
negative). The handshake is a positive image — something that existed, that worked,
that was lost when commerce scaled beyond the social group.

---

## Key precisions

**Institutional coordination cost, not transaction cost.** Payment processing is a
small cost (Stripe: 2.9%). The large cost is organizational coordination: the firm,
the platform, the escrow, the HR department, the dispute resolution layer. Figaro
doesn't compete with Stripe. It competes with the firm as a coordination mechanism.
Call it what it is.

**TCP/IP lands as a conclusion, not an opening claim.** The argument structure should
deliver the label after the audience has reasoned their way to it:

1. Cryptoeconomic systems replace coercion with mechanism-produced cooperation.
2. We applied that principle not to money or compute but to trade itself.
3. The result is a coordination protocol that sits underneath everything, cares about
   none of the details, and enforces only the structure of the deal.
4. *That* is the TCP/IP of trade.

In that order, the label lands as a conclusion. As an opening, it's a claim to accept
on faith.

---

## One sentence

Figaro is a cryptoeconomic system that applies the coordination-mechanism insight of
Bitcoin and Ethereum to trade itself — two composing mechanisms (asymmetric bonding,
which makes defection always cost more than cooperation; and buyer dominance with
atomic resolution, which enforces coordination across all sellers simultaneously)
do what leadership, escrow, and platforms do today, so that any N parties can
transact with mathematical certainty, permissionlessly, in any jurisdiction.
