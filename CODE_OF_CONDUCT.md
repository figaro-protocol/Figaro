# Code of Conduct

Figaro is a protocol project. Contribution is technical, the bar is
correctness, and the discourse should match: precise, evidence-based, and free
of personal hostility.

## Expected

- **Engage with the substance.** A claim about the kernel, a clause, or a
  proof is judged on whether it holds — not on who made it.
- **Disagree on the merits.** "This breaks invariant X, here is the trace" is
  a contribution. "This is stupid" is not.
- **Assume good faith** until the record shows otherwise.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks — in any repository surface:
  issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, commit messages.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments on the basis of identity.
- Sustained disruption of technical discussion.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Scope

This applies to every contribution surface of the repository and to everyone
participating in them — contributors, reviewers, and the maintainer alike.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern **privately to the project maintainer through
GitHub**. Do not raise conduct reports in public issues, pull requests,
reviews, or discussions — a public surface is the wrong place for them.

Reports are read by the maintainer, who may warn a participant, remove
contributions, or block a participant from the repository, in proportion to
the conduct. The maintainer is held to the same standard; a concern about the
maintainer is reported the same way.
